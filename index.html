Questions

About affordable proxy plans.

What is the lowest SocksNode residential proxy rate?

Standard self-serve starts at $0.90/GB for 5 GB. Residential volume contracts for orders above 10 TB start at $0.30/GB. The volume price is not the self-serve rate.

What is the lowest SocksNode datacenter proxy rate?

Standard self-serve starts at $0.61/GB for 5 GB. Datacenter volume contracts for orders above 10 TB start at $0.18/GB.

Is mobile Basic really about $1.33 a day?

Yes. The 30-day Basic plan costs $40, which is about $1.33/day, with 15 GB/day included per port. The 2-hour Basic trial costs $1. The US-only Economy plan is $30 for 30 days, or $1/day.

How many mobile modems are in the plans?

The pricing page snapshot lists access to 289 Basic, 144 Economy and 33 Turbo modems. Counts describe plan pools, may overlap across tiers, and do not guarantee a modem is free in every location. Check the dashboard for live availability.

Can I change mobile IP and location?

IP changes are available on demand at no extra charge. Country, US state and carrier switching is also free where supported; live availability depends on tier and location.

Where can I check live prices?

Use the pricing overview or each linked product page. The live page controls if a price or availability changes.
