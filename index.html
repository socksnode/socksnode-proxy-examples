Questions

About affordable proxy plans.

What is the cheapest SocksNode proxy plan?

Entry prices depend on the billing unit: datacenter starts at $0.61/GB, residential at $0.90/GB, mobile has a $1 two-hour Basic trial, and static ISP rental starts at $1.76 per IP for seven days. Check the live page for availability.

Are residential and datacenter data packages subscriptions?

No. Both are prepaid bandwidth packages and the purchased bandwidth does not expire. Mobile and static ISP products are time-based rentals.

What is included with the mobile proxy trial?

The Basic two-hour trial starts at $1 and includes the base daily data allowance of 15 GB per port. Current location and tier options appear on the mobile product page.

Where can I compare all SocksNode plans?

Visit the pricing overview or select a product above for its current price, availability and limits.
